Support

Answers to the common questions about the watch faces. If yours isn’t here, email me.

How do I install a watch face?

On your phone, open the watch face on Google Play, tap the arrow next to Install and choose your watch. You can also install it from Google Play on the watch itself. Then long-press your current watch face, swipe to the end and tap Add.

How do I change colours and complications?

Long-press the watch face and tap Edit. Swipe between pages to choose a colour theme and the complications for each slot. On Galaxy watches you can also do this in the Galaxy Wearable app.

Which watches are supported?

Any watch with Wear OS 5 or newer and a round screen, for example recent Pixel Watch and Galaxy Watch models. Google Play only offers a face if your watch can run it.

Why do complications show “--”?

When the watch is locked, for example after you take it off, Wear OS hides complication data for privacy and shows dashes. Unlock the watch and the data comes back. The time, date and battery stay visible.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Within 48 hours of buying, you can request a refund directly in Google Play. After that, email me with your order number and I’ll help.

Still stuck?

Tell me your watch model, its Wear OS version and which watch face it is about.
