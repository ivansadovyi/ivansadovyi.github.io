Privacy policy

This policy covers the watch faces Pulse, Orbit, Segment, Arcs, Giant, Contour, Stride, Bauhaus, Matrix, Wordsmith and Terminal, and this website. They are published by Ivan Sadovyi, a sole proprietorship registered in Poland (NIP 9452316278), ul. Szlak 77/222, 31-153 Kraków, Poland.

IN SHORT

My watch faces don’t collect personal data, don’t send anything to a server and don’t share anything with third parties. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Watch faces

The watch faces are built with Google’s Watch Face Format. They contain no executable code and have no internet access. They display information your watch already has, such as the time, battery level, steps, heart rate, weather or your next event, through the system’s complications. That information stays on your watch: the watch face cannot send it anywhere, and I never receive it.

Your colour and complication choices are saved on the watch by Wear OS and are removed when you remove the watch face.

Purchases and Google Play

The watch faces are sold through Google Play, which processes your payment under the Google Privacy Policy. I never see your payment details. Google gives me limited order information, such as the order number, product, country and price, which I keep for accounting for as long as Polish tax law requires.

Google Play also shows me aggregated statistics (installs, ratings, countries) and, if you chose to share usage and diagnostics with Google, anonymous crash reports. Google collects this data, not the watch faces, and it does not identify you to me.

This website

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Emails and children

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and delete them once they are no longer needed. The watch faces are not directed at children under 13 and collect no data from anyone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask me for access to, correction of or deletion of any personal data I hold about you, which in practice means only emails you sent me. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
